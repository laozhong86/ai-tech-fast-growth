## 🛠️ Two Core Engineering Mechanisms

### Mechanism 1: 3-Angle Viral Advisor (Never guess in the dark)
When given a news link, the pipeline immediately drafts **3 distinctive strategic angles**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               💡 3-Angle Viral Strategy Advisor        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Angle A [Monetization] : How solo operators leverage this to 10x output │
│ Angle B [Counter-Intuitive] : Why tech giants got it wrong; the hidden truth │
│ Angle C [Disruption]  : Industry reshuffle countdown; legacy models obsolete │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
Pick with one letter (`A`, `B`, or `C`), and the pipeline instantly crafts the matched pace and copy.

---

### Mechanism 2: 5-Dimension Retention Radar (Audit every render)
Before final export, every video undergoes automated quality gating:

* **1. Golden 3-Second Hook**: Dual-line headline under 10 chars per line with warm-gold gradient;
* **2. Frame-Zero Viability**: Zero black-screen fade-ins; optimal brightness for thumbnail feed CTR;
* **3. Anti-Machine Copywriting**: Native colorful Emoji (⚡ 💡 🎯) list starters; strictly ban generic pill tags;
* **4. Clean Screen Margin**: Bottom area strictly clean with zero watermark or creator disclaimers;
* **5. Short-Loop Retention**: Controlled strictly within 5–13 seconds to trigger repeat loop plays.
